My Top 5 Favourite Meals

  1. Porridge and Efo riro

    A delicious combination of hearty, comforting porridge served with rich, flavorful Efo Riro, packed with fresh vegetables, spices, and delicious proteins.

  2. Iyan and Egusi Soup

    Soft, smooth pounded yam served with rich and delicious Egusi soup, packed with flavorful spices, vegetables, and tasty proteins.

  3. Amala with Ewedu and Gbegiri Soup

    Soft, smooth Amala served with silky Ewedu and rich, flavorful Gbegiri soup, creating a delicious and satisfying Nigerian classic.

  4. Jollof Rice with Chicken

    A classic West African dish of flavorful, spicy Jollof rice served with tender, juicy chicken, creating a delicious and satisfying meal.

  5. Ewa Agan and Bread

    A simple yet delicious combination of creamy, flavorful Ewa Agan (stewed beans) served with soft, fresh bread, creating a satisfying and comforting meal.
