Brief History of Osun State University (UNIOSUN)

Osun State University (UNIOSUN) is a public university established by the Osun State Government in December 2006 under the administration of Prince Olagunsoye Oyinlola.
The university received approval from the National Universities Commission (NUC) on 21 December 2006 and commenced full academic activities on 21 September 2007.
The university has its main campus in Osogbo, the capital of Osun State, and operates across six campuses in the state.

UNIOSUN is committed to academic excellence, research, practical skills development, and community service.
Its motto is “Living Spring of Knowledge and Culture,” reflecting its dedication to learning, innovation, and cultural development.
